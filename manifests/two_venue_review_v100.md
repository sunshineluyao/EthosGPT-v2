# Reviewer-style assessment — FAST and GlobalSouthAI

## Overall assessment

The revision now presents a credible strong-acceptance case for GlobalSouthAI
and an acceptance-leaning case for FAST. The recommendation remains a reviewer
judgment, not a guaranteed outcome. The venue-neutral manuscript satisfies the
shared four-page short-paper constraint, preserves anonymous NeurIPS formatting,
and moves technical detail to a reviewable appendix.

## FAST perspective

**Recommendation: accept-leaning.**

The strongest FAST contribution is the exact finite-five decomposition for a
homogeneous population of model outputs. It supplies a technically checkable
pre-interaction diagnostic: replication reduces run-varying error but leaves the
shared cultural residual. That result is paired with dependence-aware country
inference, a joint max-T family, and a direct research path to communication,
heterogeneous roles, and model diversity. Figure 2 now supports this argument
with distinct idioms instead of redundant panels.

The principal residual FAST risk is scope: the current data do not observe
interactive agents or emergent norms. The revision handles this honestly in the
title, abstract, formal identity, discussion, and claim ledger. A reviewer who
requires deployed multi-agent interaction may still view the work as a foundation
rather than a complete agent-systems experiment, but the paper no longer invites
an overclaim objection.

## GlobalSouthAI perspective

**Recommendation: strong-acceptance case.**

The paper makes global coverage auditable while refusing to treat countries as
uniform cultures. English country prompting is explicitly framed as a stress
test; the operational consequence is multilingual, within-country, and locally
governed validation before consequential deployment. External map labels avoid
territorial-region claims, and the three error classes are defined transparently.
The paper also connects technical evidence to practical release gates rather than
stopping at a benchmark score.

The principal residual GlobalSouthAI risk is that the evidence still uses an
English prompt and an unweighted public survey derivative. Those limits are
prominent, sensitivity-tested where possible, and converted into a concrete next
study rather than hidden. The archived and published benchmarks are retained as
context but are visibly segregated from the new 64-country experiment.

## Cross-venue strengths

- Exact four-page main body; references start on page 5.
- One memorable thesis: a model update is a measurable cultural intervention.
- One reusable technical object: the versioned, paired update audit.
- One bounded FAST bridge: shared residual mismatch before agent interaction.
- One bounded economics bridge: declared creative-destruction margins and future
  causal tests, not claimed welfare effects.
- One practical GlobalSouthAI bridge: local validation as a release gate.
- Full offline reconstruction from 3,840 hash-validated outputs.

## Remaining reviewer questions already answered in the paper

1. **Are repeated generations independent observations?** No; country is the
   inferential unit and repeated generations are averaged within cell.
2. **Does lower TVD imply universal improvement?** No; W1 and geometry remain
   uncertain, and country/item heterogeneity is visible.
3. **Are the maps causal or culturally regional?** No; they are descriptive.
4. **Are the economic weights estimated?** No; they are declared sensitivity
   scenarios.
5. **Does the agent result test interaction?** No; it is explicitly pre-interaction.
