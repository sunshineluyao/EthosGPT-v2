# Evidence-preserving humanization review — final v1.0.0 pass

## Scope

The review covered the title, abstract, all four main sections, figure captions,
technical appendices, submission metadata, reproducibility instructions, and
AI-use disclosure. It followed the public `blader/humanizer` editorial guidance
requested by the author and the project's responsible-writing rules.

## Claims held fixed

- 64 countries, six survey items, two releases, five generations per cell, and
  3,840 validated outputs;
- country-level inference, all estimates, intervals, multiplicity families,
  spatial results, and the Q121 sensitivity boundary;
- the exact finite-five averaging identity and its non-interactive meaning;
- the declared, descriptive status of the creative-destruction weight analysis;
- the English-only, unweighted, ecological, non-causal, and non-legitimacy limits.

## Revisions

1. The abstract now begins with the interdisciplinary problem, places the new
   experiment beside WorldValuesBench and the five-version GPT study, gives the
   GitHub commitment in one sentence, and ends with the intellectual and
   practical contribution followed by a future-research agenda.
2. Report-style mini-headings and sentence fragments were removed from the main
   argument. Sentence length and structure now vary without rhetorical inflation.
3. “Release gate” was removed from reader-facing prose. The discussion now states
   the intended action directly: repeat the comparison, inspect regressions and
   geometry, and obtain multilingual, within-country, community-led evidence
   before consequential adoption.
4. The measures now follow one visible chain from survey shares to item distances
   (W1/TVD), directed country profiles (CRG), and country-pair relations
   (VDR/CSR). The paper explicitly rejects a composite interpretation.
5. Report vocabulary such as “contract,” “boundary,” “ledger,” and “frontier” was
   replaced where it described prose organization rather than a necessary
   statistical or technical concept.
6. Figure 1 now reads “evidence → comparison → implications,” and its panel-B
   title and sample metadata occupy separate lanes.
7. Section 4 now presents the study boundaries as a forward research agenda
   rather than a terminal limitation list. Appendix J translates that agenda
   into seven testable paths spanning measurement, mechanisms, and impact.

## Final questions

What still sounds machine-generated? The command descriptions, model identifiers,
formula definitions, and checklist necessarily retain technical syntax. The main
argument no longer relies on slogan-like fragments, generic conclusions, or
undefined process jargon.

Did the revision alter a claim, number, or citation? No. Citation-key comparison
found no main-text changes, numerical differences were limited to removing one
redundant threshold repetition from a caption, and executable verification checks
the full reported result set.
