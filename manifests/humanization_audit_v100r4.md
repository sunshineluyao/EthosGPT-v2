# Evidence-preserving writing audit — v1.0.0 revision 6

## Reader-facing objective

The final writing pass was designed for an interdisciplinary NeurIPS workshop
reader spanning machine learning, survey measurement, innovation economics,
agent systems, and Global South AI. It revised argument order and explanation,
not the empirical evidence.

## Preserved invariants

- 64 countries, six survey items, two model versions, five generations per
  country–item cell, and 3,840 validated outputs;
- country as the independent inferential unit;
- the global TVD contrast and uncertainty, the inconclusive global W1, CRG,
  VDR, and CSR contrasts, and the joint 12-outcome conclusions;
- the exact finite-five averaging identity and its pre-interaction boundary;
- the descriptive status of the creative-destruction weight surface;
- the English-only, unweighted, ecological, non-causal, and non-legitimacy
  boundaries, including the Q121 protocol conflict.

## Structural revisions

1. The abstract now moves through shared problem, literature gap, research
   question, paired design, principal evidence, boundary, intellectual
   contribution, practical use, and open science.
2. Section 1 names one update-audit research question and three distinct
   contributions, then connects them to economic, agent-system, and deployment
   applications.
3. Section 2 defines W1, TVD, CRG, VDR, and CSR before any result, pairing each
   formula with intuition, target, contrast direction, and an appendix trace.
4. Section 3 is organized by three scientific questions: average fidelity,
   concentration of gains, and persistence under repetition.
5. Section 4 separates current evidence from future causal, interactive-agent,
   multilingual, within-country, and community-governed tests.
6. The appendix now opens with the open-science access point and a
   reader-question roadmap.

## Result

Pass. The prose is less report-like and more explanatory without changing any
number, denominator, uncertainty statement, measure, or non-claim. Human
authors must review and accept the wording and replace the pending repository
access line after anonymized upload.
