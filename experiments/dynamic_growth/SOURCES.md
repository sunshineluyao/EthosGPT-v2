The companion research design and sample archive specify the reduced fold model and three teaching examples. All eight sample data outputs were independently reproduced. This extension implements full marginal distributions, measured-error propagation, cultural dynamics, no-fold controls, explicit equal-spending rollout, and the closed-resource benchmark. The supplied guide remains a research design; robust control, empirical calibration, and longitudinal identification are not represented as completed results.

The representation input provenance, prompt conflicts, source-coding sensitivity, human-derivative limitations, and statistical methods remain those of the frozen original audit. This study uses aggregate probabilities and introduces no additional provider or participant data.
