\section{Creative destruction, agent systems, and responsible adoption}

The economic analysis asks how representational error is distributed across the choices
that accompany technological change. Squared TVD falls for opportunity and participation
($-0.0047$, Holm $p=0.0035$) and most for distribution and adjustment ($-0.0213$,
$p<0.001$), while coordination and legitimacy remain uncertain ($+0.0024$, $p=0.067$).
Across 1,326 nonnegative weight combinations, 88.76\% of intervals favor GPT-5.6, 11.24\%
are uncertain, and none favor GPT-5.5. This pre-declared sensitivity analysis does not
estimate 1,326 separate effects or welfare; it motivates causal work on adoption,
entrepreneurship, labor adjustment, compensation, and political legitimacy.

Uniformly averaging subsets from the five observed outputs reduces run-varying error but
leaves the shared residual $E_5$. Because the outputs neither communicate nor act, the
identity makes no claim about collective behavior. It instead gives future agent
experiments a baseline for asking whether voting, deliberation, feedback, or heterogeneous
roles change the mismatch already present before interaction.

Naming a country in English can conceal variation in language, region, class, and
institutions, so geographic coverage does not establish inclusion
\citep{nekoto2020participatory,mohamed2020decolonial,sen1999development}. Before adopting
an updated model for consequential use, developers and institutions should repeat the
comparison, examine country--item regressions and geometry changes, and seek multilingual
evidence from within-country experts and affected communities. No community partner
co-designed or locally validated this wave, so it cannot establish local legitimacy. The
open materials make the comparison repeatable as models and community priorities change.

The evidence covers six English survey items, two releases, five generations, an
unweighted public derivative, and one wording conflict. These limits define future
research: weighted, multilingual, and within-country surveys can recover linguistic and
social heterogeneity; longitudinal and interactive-agent designs can trace change across
releases and collective behavior; and community-led field studies can connect the
measures to locally legitimate adoption and economic adjustment.
Appendix~\ref{app:future-roadmap} details this roadmap. Until then, lower category-mass
error should not imply preserved item ordering, country geometry, welfare, or local
legitimacy.
