GPT-5.6 has lower global TVD error ($\Delta=-0.0080$, 95\% BCa CI
$[-0.0107,-0.0050]$, Holm $p<0.001$; Table~\ref{tab:main}). The ordered W1 change is
smaller and uncertain ($-0.0020$, CI $[-0.0040,0.0003]$), and CRG is nearly unchanged.
VDR moves toward the human spread target ($0.587\rightarrow0.609$) while CSR falls
($0.321\rightarrow0.288$); neither target-loss contrast is conclusive. Thus lower
category-mass error does not imply uniformly better country representation.

Within one 12-outcome family, responsibility improves under W1 ($-0.0082$,
simultaneous CI $[-0.0156,-0.0009]$, max-$T$ $p=0.017$) and TVD ($-0.0207$,
$[-0.0301,-0.0113]$, $p<0.001$). Income distribution improves under TVD
($-0.0266$, $[-0.0389,-0.0143]$, $p<0.001$), whereas its W1 result remains borderline
after joint correction ($p=0.059$). No other item passes this familywise test; the
disclosed Q121 conflict is not a corrected worsening.

A five-run mean removes only 1.18\% of GPT-5.5 and 1.31\% of GPT-5.6 single-run squared
probability error (Figure~\ref{fig:results}b). The GPT-5.6 five-run residual is lower by
$0.0063$ (95\% CI $[-0.0083,-0.0045]$), while run-varying components do not differ
reliably. Spatial-error estimates over $k=4,6,8$ graphs and spatial-HAC intervals over
1,000--5,000 km keep the global TVD interval below zero. TVD changes are not spatially
clustered after diagnostic correction; CRG retains a local Geary signal. These checks
support the average contrast, not diffusion or spillover.
