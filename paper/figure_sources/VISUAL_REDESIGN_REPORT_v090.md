# Visual redesign report — v0.9.0

## Outcome

Figure 1 is now a nested argument rather than a stand-alone map: prior published and
archived benchmarks ground the current 64-country update evidence, which then connects
to measured creative-destruction margins and an explicitly prospective economics
research program. Figure 2 uses idioms chosen from the INFOSCI 301 decision tree for
the actual reading tasks: paired values, a distribution with observations, ordered
run change, simultaneous intervals, and shared-axis profiles.

## Implemented corrections

| Requested issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Map labels and arrows overlapped; a sensitive place was used as an example. | All six country names are outside the maps with arrowheads terminating at the mapped point. The examples are India/Germany/South Korea for TVD and Colombia/United States/Kenya for CRG. |
| Lower, near-zero, and higher error were not defined. | For each metric, $\tau=0.1\max|\Delta|$. Lower is $\Delta<-\tau$, near zero is $|\Delta|\leq\tau$, and higher is $\Delta>\tau$. The resulting thresholds are 0.0030 for TVD and 0.0225 for CRG. |
| Figure 2 dumbbells lacked a visible joining line. | A dark connector segment is drawn from every GPT-5.5 endpoint to its paired GPT-5.6 endpoint before the endpoint markers. |
| Figure 2 mixed reader tasks without an explicit idiom rationale. | The panels are now a connected dumbbell, three-type raincloud, run slope graph, simultaneous interval forest, and aligned profile chart. The radar was removed because the shared-axis aligned profile supports more reliable comparison. |
| Earlier benchmark results had disappeared from the main story. | Figure 1 includes the four published WorldValuesBench success shares, the protocol-separated archived four-model CRG reanalysis, and the PNAS Nexus five-version/country-prompting result. Full details are in Appendix I. |
| Creative destruction was too peripheral. | Figure 1 includes the three observed representation-loss margins and a dashed box for future causal tests of adoption/entrepreneurship, labor adjustment/compensation, and legitimacy/coordination. Sections 1 and 4 explain the evidence boundary. |
| Figure 1 needed to remain editable while combining Python charts. | `fig1_spatial_story.drawio` is the structural master. It embeds three live-vector SVG child charts regenerated by `scripts/make_visual_story_v090.py`; the final publication PDF/SVG remains vector-only. |

## Idiom decision record

The decision-tree branch used for each Figure 2 panel is recorded below. The reference
was the supplied “Idiom Algorithm Preview” notebook.

| Panel | Reading task | Decision-tree idiom | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | Compare paired model values and gaps | Dumbbell | One common scale, two values per domain, explicit connectors. |
| b | Compare distribution, summary, and observations | Raincloud | Density, quartiles/median, and all 64 country changes remain visible. |
| c | Compare ordered change over $K=1,\ldots,5$ | Slope graph | Emphasizes the change in error remaining as observed runs are averaged. |
| d | Compare estimates with uncertainty | Interval/forest plot | Simultaneous intervals and corrected marker fill are primary. |
| e | Compare many measures on common axes | Aligned profiles | All six dimensions share a 0--1 scale; common horizontal axes avoid radar-angle judgments. |
